# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system designed to make food ordering easier for students and more manageable for campus food providers. Students can view available food items, place orders, and track their orders. Food providers can manage their menu and process incoming orders.

The system provides a simple connection between students and campus food services. It reduces the need for manual ordering and helps users keep track of their food orders.

## Who

CampusEats has the following main users:

1. **Students** — browse food items, place orders, and track their orders.
2. **Food Providers** — manage food items, receive orders, and update order status.
3. **Administrator** — manages the overall system and users.
